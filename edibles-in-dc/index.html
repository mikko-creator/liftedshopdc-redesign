ABOUT EDIBLES

Edibles are a popular way to consume cannabis, whether for recreational or medical purposes. They come in various forms such as gummies, chocolates, baked goods, and beverages, and offer an alternative to smoking or vaping. Edibles have a delayed onset time compared to inhalation methods, and it can take anywhere from 30 minutes to two hours to feel the full effects. It is important to start small and wait at least two hours before consuming more to avoid overconsumption and potential unwanted side effects.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN SMOKING CANNABIS AND EATING EDIBLES?

When smoking cannabis, the effects are felt almost instantly because the THC enters the bloodstream through the lungs. On the other hand, edibles must first pass through the digestive system before the liver metabolizes them into the active compound, THC. This process can take longer to feel the effects, but the effects can last much longer than smoking.

WHAT IS A HIGH-POTENCY EDIBLE?

High potency edibles contain high percentages of THC. THC is the psychoactive compound in cannabis that is responsible for the “high” associated with the plant. While some people prefer low-THC or CBD-only products, others enjoy the full effects of THC.

FAQ

ARE EDIBLES SAFE TO CONSUME?

Edibles can be safe to consume as long as they are consumed responsibly and in moderation. It is crucial to purchase edibles from a reputable source that uses high-quality ingredients and accurately labels their products’ potency. It is also essential to start with a low dosage and wait for the effects to take hold before consuming more.

HOW MANY EDIBLES SHOULD I EAT?

Start with a low dose, usually between 5-10mg of THC per serving, and wait at least two hours before consuming more. It is important to note that individual tolerance levels can vary widely, so it is always best to start with a small amount and gradually increase based on personal experience.

CAN EDIBLES BE USED FOR MEDICAL PURPOSES?

Yes, edibles can be used for medical purposes such as pain management, anxiety relief, and reducing inflammation. However, it is important to consult with a medical professional before using edibles for medical reasons.

ARE THERE ANY SIDE EFFECTS WHEN CONSUMING EDIBLES?

Like any substance, there can be side effects associated with consuming edibles. Common side effects include dry mouth, red eyes, increased heart rate, and impaired coordination. These side effects can be avoided by consuming edibles responsibly and in moderation.

CAN I DRIVE AFTER CONSUMING EDIBLES?

No, it is not recommended to drive after consuming edibles. The effects of edibles can impair cognitive function and motor skills, making driving unsafe. It is important to wait until the effects of the edibles have worn off before operating a vehicle or heavy machinery.

DO EDIBLES EXPIRE?

Yes, edibles can expire, just like any food item. It is essential to check the expiration date when purchasing edibles. Be sure to store them properly to ensure they remain fresh and effective.
