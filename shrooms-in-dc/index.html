ABOUT PSILOCYBIN

Psilocybin is a psychoactive compound found in certain species of mushrooms which produce hallucinogenic effects when consumed. It has been used for centuries by indigenous cultures in Central and South America for ceremonial and religious purposes. (1) It has also been used recreationally for many hundreds of years.

Although the use of magic mushrooms has a long history, little was really known about their scientific makeup until the middle of the 20th century. Swiss chemist Albert Hoffman first identified and synthesized the psychoactive compounds in psilocybe mushrooms in the 1950s, prompting further research into their properties. However, after the psychedelics explosion in the 1960s and 70s, psilocybin was classified under Schedule I by the US Drug Enforcement Agency. (2)

In recent years, there has been a growing interest in the potential therapeutic uses of these fungi, with some states decriminalizing their use and others allowing the production and sale of edibles. In fact an estimated 5.5 million Americans use hallucinogens every year! (3)

COMMON PSILOCYBIN MUSHROOM SPECIES

  • Psilocybe cubensis (“cubes”)
  • Psilocybe semilanceata (“liberty cap”)
  • Psilocybe azurescens (“flying saucers”)
  • Psilocybin tampanensis (“philosopher’s stone”)
  • Panaeolus cyanescens (“blue meanies”)
  • Psilocybe zapotecorum (“badao”)
  • Psilocybe mexicana (“teonanacatl”)

HOW DOES PSILOCYBIN WORK?

Psilocybin works by binding to serotonin receptors in the brain, triggering changes in brain chemistry. This can lead to altered states of consciousness, heightened emotions, and a feeling of connection with nature, among other possible effects. The therapeutic effects of psilocybin are still being studied, however some studies have shown that it can be useful in treating a variety of conditions including depression, anxiety, (4) PTSD, and addiction.

WHAT DOES PSILOCYBIN FEEL LIKE?

Although every individual’s experience is different, there are some common effects you can expect when you’re using shroom-based products. A good rule to follow is to be open-minded and understanding of the effects of your chosen goods beforehand.

MENTAL EFFECTS

  • You might feel extreme waves of happiness (euphoria) while you are using psilocybin.
  • You might feel like you have a spiritual connection with things. This is sometimes known as a “mystical” or “transcendental” experience.
  • You might see things or hear sounds that aren’t really there.
  • You might feel you have a deeper relationship with nature and the Earth.
  • You might lose track of time, or perceive time, space, and reality differently.
  • You might feel more panicked or paranoid than normal.

PHYSICAL EFFECTS

  • Your pupils might be much bigger.
  • You might feel more drowsy.
  • Your heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature might increase.
  • You might not have as much control over your body movements.
  • You might get a headache.
  • You might feel nauseous.

STAY SAFE, STAY COMFORTABLE, & ENJOY YOUR TRIP!

USING SHROOMS

If you’re looking to get started with using magic mushrooms, there are a few different ways to go about it.

AS IS OR COOKED

The most common and well-studied types of magic mushrooms have a distinct taste. This includes species such as Psilocybe cubensis, Psilocybe semilanceata, and Psilocybe mexicana. Their taste is often described as slightly earthy or nutty. They can be eaten raw or cooked, but most people find it easier to eat them combined with something else.

SHROOM TEA

If you want to avoid the unique taste of raw mushrooms, making tea is a great way to enjoy the effects. To make mushroom tea, simply mix a mushroom tea bag or mushroom powder with hot water and steep for 15 minutes. You may also add sugar, honey, or lemon to mask the taste further. If possible, let your tea cool before drinking it so that the active ingredients are not destroyed by the heat.

SHROOM CHOCOLATE

It used to be a messy affair if you wanted to make shroom chocolate. Now, thanks to certain DC dispensaries like us, there’s no need to melt chocolate and mix it with shrooms in molds, as the sweet treats come pre-packaged and ready to eat! Now you can buy mushroom chocolates you can measure out by the piece. Be sure to gauge how much you should be taking at once.

SHROOM POWDER

If you want to get the full effects of magic mushrooms, one of the best options is to buy powdered mushrooms. Add them to your favorite drinks, snacks, and food. Powdered mushrooms are available from select vendors, like Lifted DC, and come in a range of potencies. When using powdered mushrooms, start with a low dose and increase gradually until you reach the desired effect.

SHROOM TINCTURE

Tinctures are an easy and discreet way to enjoy the effects of magic mushrooms without having to consume large amounts of powder. Tinctures can have a powerful effect, so start with a small dose and work your way up.

FAQ

ARE SHROOMS LEGAL IN WASHINGTON DC?

Yes, psilocybin has been decriminalized in Washington DC.

WHAT ARE SOME OF THE BENEFITS OF USING MAGIC MUSHROOMS?

Many people report feeling more connected to nature, increased creativity, improved emotional balance, and enhanced spirituality. Some also report reduced anxiety, improved focus, and improved sleep.

ARE THERE ANY LONG-TERM SIDE EFFECTS OF USING MAGIC MUSHROOMS?

Most of the short-term side effects of magic mushrooms will disappear shortly after the trip ends. However, in rare cases, people may experience lingering psychological effects, such as flashbacks, depression, or anxiety. It is important to be aware of these potential risks before consuming magic mushrooms.
