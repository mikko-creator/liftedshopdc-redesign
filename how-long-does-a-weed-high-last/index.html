How Long Does A Weed High Last?

ORDER DELIVERY(771) 216-1684

by Lifted Shop

Both new and seasoned weed users want to know how long a weed high lasts. The reason for this might be because your high doesn’t last the same as that of other users you know or, maybe it doesn’t last as long as you thought it should. But, in the same breath, as a routine user, you might have noticed your high is shorter or longer than that of other people.

Whatever reasons compelling you to seek the facts about the duration of this high are justified. However, for starters, the best authority to tell us what weed or marijuana is, is the World Health Organization (WHO). According to WHO, weed is a general term that refers to products that trace their origin to the cannabis Sativa plant.

You can use the products of this plant for both medicinal and recreational purposes. Additionally, weed, or marijuana, contains diverse compounds referred to as cannabinoids. The amount of these compounds in each plant is dependent on the strain of cannabis in question.

However, amongst the many types of cannabinoids available in the different strains of cannabis, the two most common are cannabidiol (CBD) and tetrahydrocannabinol (THC). Therefore, the type of high you experience from weed depends on the mixture and composition of these two compounds in the product you are using.

Tetrahydrocannabinol, or THC, is the primary psychoactive compound responsible for high and more mind-altering results.

WHAT DETERMINES HOW LONG DOES BEING HIGH LAST?

Several factors determine the duration of the high you get from weed. These factors include

  • The strain or strain of weed.
  • Your characteristics, such as tolerance and metabolism.
  • The method you use to consume weed, for instance, smoking or edibles.
  • Whether or not you take other substances, such as alcohol.

So if you are wondering how long does weed lasts, consider the following factors one by one.

TYPE OF WEED

The gender of the cannabis plant used to make the product you are using is one of the factors affecting how long the high you get lasts. Moreover, whether the type of cannabis used contains seeds or not will affect the duration of the length of the high you experience.

The high you get depends mainly on the CBD and THC content. Weed is more potent in the form of hashish and sinsemilla. This higher potency enables this kind of weed to create more extended highs.

Growers of cannabis have become aware of this fact. For that reason, they have been increasing the amount of tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) in their plants over the years. So don’t be surprised that modern-day weed leaves trigger a more potent and longer high than their previous variations.

YOUR CHARACTERISTICS

We are all different. Our bodies react differently to other compounds, and weed is not different. Consider critical areas that we differ on and how it affects the length of the high that one experiences: metabolism and tolerance.

METABOLISM

Your rate of metabolism affects the length of the high you get from weed. We all have different rates of metabolism. If you have a high metabolic rate, this means your body will process cannabis much faster than that of the one with a slower metabolic rate. As a result, THC will reach your bloodstream more quickly than that of a person with a slower metabolism.

However, this does not mean the high duration will be longer. Instead, it means you will experience a high faster than the other person.

CANNABIS TOLERANCE

If you ingest weed more frequently, you are likely to have a greater tolerance to this substance than your friends who don’t. Higher tolerance for cannabis means its effects on you are weaker and will not last as long.

DIET

We don’t eat the same foods. However, some foods you eat might contain that can enhance and extend your cannabis high. For instance, anecdotal reports imply that mangoes can yield these results. In contrast, foods rich in fat can reduce the effects of weed.

THE METHOD OF CONSUMPTION

How you consume cannabis will also affect how long your peaks will last.

According to Drugs and Me, a website by the respected Mental Health Education Foundation:

  • Smoking or vaping. The effects will peak around 10 minutes after consuming weed and will typically last 1 to 3 hours. However, the high can linger for up to 8 hours.
  • Dabbing. The effects of dabbing last 1 to 3 hours. If you are using a high THC concentrate, you might feel the impact for a whole day.
  • Eating. The highs of edibles usually peak about 2 hours after you consume weed this way and can last up to 24 hours.

WHETHER YOU TAKE OTHER SUBSTANCES SUCH AS ALCOHOL

According to studies published by Pub Med Central, alcohol and cannabis use go hand in hand. When used together, the two boost each other’s effects. Taking alcohol alongside cannabis also causes the THC to remain in your body’s system for longer than it should. THC stays in your body’s system longer than it should because alcohol slows down your body’s metabolic process, which, in turn, extends the duration of your high.

HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO KICK IN

The same factors highlighted above affect how long it takes for your high to kick in. How you use cannabis is the main factor influencing when THC levels in your bloodstream reach their peak.

  • Inhaling vapor is the quickest way to get high to effect. This method delivers higher peak THC concentrations in your bloodstream.
  • Smoking cannabis comes second in yielding a quick high. You’ll get high within minutes.
  • Edibles deliver the slowest high, taking 30–60 minutes to occur.

HOW LONG DOES A HIGH LAST?

Several factors can influence your peak. For example, weed can trigger several uncomfortable effects such as anxiety and paranoia. If these effects show up, or you wish to reduce the effects of THC for another reason, there are some methods you might find helpful.

Try the following:

Taking a cold shower or bath can increase a person’s alertness and thus reduce the high. Research suggests that foods such as pine nuts, lemon, and black pepper- which contain terpenoid compounds- can serve as effective antidotes to a weed high. Also, the sourness of lemons, together with the fruit’s terpenoid content, can reduce the high by shocking the person into becoming more alert. However, the best way to shed off the effects of THC is to sleep.

WHAT CAN EXTEND THE HIGH?

The method you chose to get high affects how long the effects last. Consuming edibles will not trigger these effects as fast as other methods, but it will prolong the high. The more cannabis you consume, the longer your body metabolizes the THC, and the more extended the high you get.

PRECAUTIONS

The use of cannabis for recreation is a controversial subject and illegal in many states. Thus, you should ensure the cannabis you intend to use is purchased legally from recognized and licensed vendors like LIFTED DC Dispensary in DC.

On the same note, bear in mind that weed comes with short- and long-term risk factors, meaning you should consume cannabis products with care.

Short-term risks include:

  • paranoia
  • increased in heart rate
  • anxiety

Long-term risks include:

  • insomnia
  • memory issues
  • difficulty learning
  • schizophrenia, especially if you have a higher genetic risk of the condition

If you are pregnant, using cannabis can lead to complications associated with fetal development.

WHEN TO SEE YOUR DOCTOR

You should see your doctor if you become dependent on weed. Also, if you experience side effects, such as memory loss or insomnia, it’s time to seek professional help.
