ABOUT CONCENTRATES

Cannabis concentrates are a popular and effective way to consume cannabis, as the effects can be felt almost immediately and can last for several hours. They offer a more potent high compared to smoking traditional cannabis flower. (1) This means that you’ll need less product to achieve the desired effect, making it a more cost-effective option in the long run. Additionally, because concentrates are made by extracting THC and other cannabinoids from the plant material, they offer a purer and cleaner experience.

When choosing a concentrate, it’s important to consider its potency and the method of consumption. There are several types of concentrates available, including shatter, wax, crumble, and live resin. Each type has its own unique texture and consistency, but all provide a concentrated dose of THC.

EXTRACTION METHODS

There are several methods used to extract cannabinoids and terpenes from cannabis plants, including butane hash oil (BHO), CO2 extraction, and rosin pressing. Each method has its advantages and disadvantages, and the resulting concentrate can vary in quality and purity.

SOLVENT-BASED CONCENTRATES
BHO and propane hash oil (PHO) are examples of solvent-based concentrates. They are produced by using a hydrocarbon such as butane or propane to extract cannabinoids and terpenes from the plant material. The resulting concentrate can be further processed through techniques like winterization and purging to remove any residual solvents and impurities. Solvent-based concentrates include:

  • CO2 Oil
  • Wax/Budder
  • Rick Simpson Oil (RSO)
  • Live Resin
  • Shatter
  • Live Resin
  • Distillate
  • Caviar
  • Crumble
  • Sugar/Crystalline

SOLVENTLESS CONCENTRATES

Solventless concentrates are produced without the use of any chemicals or solvents. Examples include rosin, a concentrate made by using heat and pressure to extract cannabinoids and terpenes from cannabis flower, and dry sift kief, which is produced by sifting trichomes from the plant material. (2) Other types of non-solvent cannabis concentrates include traditional charas and hashish, used in India and Central Asia for medicinal purposes for many hundreds of years.

ADVICE FOR BEGINNERS

Some concentrates, such as shatter and wax, are highly potent and are best used by experienced users. Others, such as distillate and CO2 oil, are less potent and can be used by beginners. Before trying cannabis concentrates, there are a few important things to keep in mind to ensure a positive and safe experience.

  • Start with low doses: Cannabis concentrates are extremely potent, so it’s important to start with small doses and work your way up gradually.
  • Choose a reputable source: Make sure you’re getting your concentrates from a trusted and licensed dispensary or supplier.
  • Know your tolerance: If you’re new to cannabis, or if you haven’t used it in a while, your tolerance may be lower than you think. Take it slow and listen to your body.
  • Be mindful of consumption methods: Concentrates can be consumed in a variety of ways, including vaping and dabbing. Make sure you understand the different methods and choose one that works best for you.
  • Stay hydrated: Cannabis can cause dry mouth, so make sure you have plenty of water on hand to stay hydrated.
  • Never drive under the influence: Just like alcohol, it’s never safe to get behind the wheel after using cannabis. Make sure you have a sober ride home.

USING A DAB RIG

Dabbing is a popular method of consuming cannabis concentrates. A dab rig is a device used for this purpose, consisting of a glass chamber with a nail, which is heated to vaporize the concentrate and create a highly potent smoke. For those new to dabbing, using a dab rig may seem intimidating, but it’s actually quite easy to use. Here are a few simple steps to follow:

  1. Fill up your rig with water. This will cool down the vapor and make it easier to inhale.
  2. Heat up the nail with a torch until it’s glowing red hot.
  3. Allow the nail to cool for around 30 seconds. This prevents the concentrate from burning and ruining the flavor.
  4. Apply a small amount of concentrate onto the nail using a dabber or similar tool.
  5. Slowly draw in the vapor while covering the carb cap hole to direct airflow over the nail.
  6. Exhale and enjoy!

FAQ

HOW ARE CANNABIS CONCENTRATES USED?

Cannabis concentrates can be used in a variety of ways, including dabbing, vaporizing, and adding to joints or bowls. Dabbing involves heating the concentrate on a hot surface and inhaling the vapor, while vaporizing involves heating the concentrate in a device and inhaling the vapor.

ARE THERE ANY RISKS ASSOCIATED WITH USING CANNABIS CONCENTRATES?

Like any cannabis product, there are risks associated with using cannabis concentrates. These include the potential for addiction, impaired judgment and coordination, and increased heart rate. It is important to consume cannabis concentrates responsibly and in moderation.

IS IT LEGAL TO PURCHASE CANNABIS CONCENTRATES?
