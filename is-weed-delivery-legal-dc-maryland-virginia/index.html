Is Weed Delivery Legal in DC, Maryland, and Virginia?

ORDER DELIVERY(771) 216-1684

If you are in the DC, Maryland, or Virginia area and wondering whether ordering cannabis delivery is legal, the short answer is yes. But the laws governing recreational cannabis vary across the three jurisdictions, and how delivery works in each one is different. This post breaks it down clearly so you know exactly where you stand before you place an order.

Is Weed Delivery Legal in Washington, DC?

Yes. Cannabis delivery is legal in Washington, DC, for adults 21 and older under Initiative 71, a voter-approved law that has been in effect since February 2015.

Initiative 71 permits the gifting of cannabis between adults 21 and older in the District. Unlike states with traditional retail dispensaries, DC does not have a licensed recreational retail framework due to Congressional restrictions on the District’s budget authority. Instead, recreational cannabis operates through a legal gifting model that has been established in the city for nearly a decade.

To receive a cannabis delivery in DC, you need to:

  • Be 21 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID
  • Have a delivery address within Washington, DC
  • Have someone 21 or older present to receive the order

There is no medical card required. No residency requirement. If you are in DC and are 21 or older, you are eligible.

For a deeper explanation of how the DC gifting model works, read: DC Weed Gifting: How Initiative 71 Works.

Is Weed Delivery Legal in Maryland?

Yes. Maryland legalized adult-use recreational cannabis in 2023, and cannabis delivery is permitted under state law for adults 21 and older.

Maryland operates a licensed dispensary model, meaning cannabis businesses must hold a state license to sell and deliver recreational cannabis. Customers do not need a medical card for recreational purchases. You need to be 21 or older with a valid ID.

Lifted Shop delivers to a wide range of Maryland addresses. Our Maryland delivery service operates under the applicable legal framework for the areas we serve. View Maryland delivery areas.

Key things to know for Maryland delivery:

  • You must be 21 or older with a valid photo ID
  • Someone 21 or older must be present to receive the delivery
  • Delivery availability depends on your specific address and proximity to our DC location
  • Cannabis purchased in Maryland must stay in Maryland and cannot be transported across state lines

Is Weed Delivery Legal in Virginia?

Yes. Virginia legalized adult-use recreational cannabis in 2021, making it one of the first states on the East Coast to do so. Retail sales and delivery are permitted for adults 21 and older.

Virginia operates a licensed retail cannabis market. Consumers do not need a medical card for recreational purchases. As with DC and Maryland, the requirements are straightforward: be 21 or older, have a valid ID, and be present to receive your order.

Lifted Shop delivers to select Virginia addresses, primarily in Northern Virginia. View Virginia delivery areas.

Key things to know for Virginia delivery:

  • You must be 21 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID
  • Someone 21 or older must be present to receive the delivery
  • Delivery availability depends on your specific address
  • Cannabis must not be transported across state lines

How Delivery Laws Differ Across DC, Maryland, and Virginia

All three jurisdictions have legalized recreational cannabis for adults 21 and older, but the legal frameworks behind them are different.

  • Washington DC operates under Initiative 71’s gifting model. There is no traditional licensed retail framework for recreational sales due to Congressional restrictions on DC’s legislative authority.
  • Maryland operates a state-licensed dispensary and retail model, established when recreational cannabis was legalized in 2023.
  • Virginia operates a state-licensed retail model, in place since adult-use cannabis was legalized in 2021.

For buyers, the practical experience is similar across all three: browse online, place your order, show your ID, and receive your cannabis. The legal structure behind each jurisdiction is different, but the process of ordering is comparable.

What is consistent everywhere: you must be 21 or older, you must have a valid ID, and cannabis cannot cross state lines. Even if you are ordering from DC into Virginia or from Maryland into DC, cannabis is for use within the jurisdiction where it was received.

Can I Order Across State Lines?

No. Regardless of legalization status in DC, Maryland, or Virginia, transporting cannabis across state lines is a federal offense. This applies even when traveling between two jurisdictions where cannabis is legal.

If you receive a delivery in DC, that cannabis must stay in DC. If you receive a delivery in Maryland, it must stay in Maryland. Do not travel across state lines with cannabis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is weed legal in DC for tourists and visitors?

Yes. DC’s Initiative 71 applies to any adult 21 or older who is physically in the District. You do not need to be a DC resident. Visitors from other states or countries can legally participate in cannabis gifting transactions in DC as long as they are 21 or older and have a valid ID.

Do I need a medical card to order in DC, Maryland, or Virginia?

No. All three jurisdictions allow recreational cannabis for adults 21 and older without a medical card. A valid government-issued photo ID confirming your age is all you need.

What ID is accepted?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID showing your date of birth. A driver’s license, passport, or state ID card all work. Expired IDs are not accepted.

Is weed delivery legal everywhere in Virginia and Maryland?

Recreational cannabis is legal statewide in both Maryland and Virginia, but delivery availability depends on your specific location and proximity to our operations. Check whether your address falls within our Maryland or Virginia delivery areas.

What if cannabis laws change?

Cannabis laws continue to evolve in this region. DC has been exploring legislation to establish a traditional retail framework, though, as of the time of writing, the gifting model remains in place.
