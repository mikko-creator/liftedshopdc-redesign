ORDER DELIVERY(771) 216-1684

Ordering weed delivery in DC is straightforward once you know how the system works. This guide walks you through the whole process from placing your first order to receiving it at your door, so you know exactly what to expect before you buy. Whether you’re a first-time buyer or new to ordering online, here’s everything you need to know.

Is Weed Delivery Legal in DC?

Yes. Cannabis delivery in Washington, DC, is legal under Initiative 71, a law passed by DC voters that permits the gifting of cannabis between adults 21 and older. When you order from a service like Lifted Shop, your transaction is structured as a lawful gift under DC law.

You do not need a medical marijuana card. You do not need a prescription. You need to be 21 or older with a valid ID.

For a full breakdown of the law and what it means for buyers, read: DC Weed Gifting: How Initiative 71 Works.

What You Need Before You Order

Before placing your first order, make sure you have the following:

  • Valid government-issued photo ID confirming you are 21 or older. A driver’s license, passport, or state ID all work.
  • A DC delivery address or a pickup plan if you’d prefer to collect in person.
  • A minimum order of $100 for same-day delivery within Washington, DC.
  • Someone 21+ present at the delivery address to receive the order and show ID.

That’s it. No membership, no account history, no referral needed.

How to Order for Pickup Instead

Prefer to collect your order rather than wait for delivery? Here’s how pickup works:

  1. Browse the menu and place your order online, the same process as delivery

  4. Show your ID and collect your order

Note: Lifted Shop does not operate as a walk-in retail store. You cannot browse products in person. All orders must be placed online before you arrive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order weed delivery if I'm visiting DC from out of state?

Yes, as long as you are 21 or older and have a valid government-issued ID. DC’s Initiative 71 applies to anyone within the District, not just residents.

What if nobody is home when the driver arrives?

Someone 21+ must be present to receive the delivery and show ID. If nobody is available, the delivery cannot be completed. Contact us to reschedule.

Is delivery available outside DC?

Yes. We deliver to select areas in Maryland and Virginia. View Maryland delivery areas and Virginia delivery areas. Delivery times and minimums may differ from DC orders.
